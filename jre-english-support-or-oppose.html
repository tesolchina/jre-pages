2025 英文卷 Set 2：支持還是反對 JRE 2025, English paper, Set 2: support or oppose

Scholaria 非本地生入讀本地中學。一份教學錄音、一份考生復盤，加上題目附帶的三份數據。以下是我的判斷，連同它的條件。 Non-local students in local secondary schools. Notes from a teaching session, a candidate's recall, and the three data items the paper supplied.

建議反對。但有一個條件：看完題後十分鐘內，你要想得出一個同級的替代方案。想不出，寫支持。 Oppose, on one condition: within ten minutes of reading the paper you can name an alternative of comparable scale. If you cannot, support.

理由不是「反對比較高分」。考試兩個立場都收。理由是這份卷的數據與題幹設計，令反對分支容易寫得成一篇文章；支持分支容易寫成三件互不相干的好主意。 Not because opposing scores better — both stances are accepted. Because with this data and this task design, the opposing branch holds together as one argument, while the supporting branch tends to fall into three unrelated good ideas.

一 · 數據文件其實是反對的材料1. The data file favours the other side

題目給三份數據：公營與私立學校的課程對比（DSE 對 IB）、非本地生的分布與時間趨勢、人口老化趨勢。多數人拿第三份寫機遇。前兩份更有用。 Three items: public vs private curriculum (DSE against IB), the distribution and trend of non-local students, and population ageing. Most candidates use the third for their opportunities. The first two are worth more.

非本地生集中在大學階段。這一句同時是現狀與論證：收外來學生的渠道已經存在，而且運作中。既然如此，要處理「人才與人口」的問題，應該擴大已通的那條渠道，而不是在中小學開一條未經測試的新渠。這個對比本身就是替代方案的骨架。 Non-locals cluster at university level. That single fact is both the status quo and the argument: the channel for attracting students from outside already exists and already works. If the aim is talent and demography, widen the working channel rather than open an untested one further down.

課程對比說明產品不對口。本地中學的主力產品是 DSE，國際家庭的期望多數是 IB 一類。想吸引人來讀中學，先要處理產品與評核，不是先改收生資格。改了資格而產品不變，來的人只會集中在少數名校，其餘學校照樣收不到生。 The curriculum chart shows a product mismatch. Local secondary schools sell DSE; international families mostly want IB or its equivalents. Changing admission rules without changing the product means the students who come concentrate in a handful of strong schools, and the rest stay empty.

老化是真問題，但時間尺度不對。中學入學到畢業再到進入勞動市場，中間是十年以上。任何一屆政府都不會用十年去證明一個政策有效。把老化當主要理由，答辯時一句「幾時見效」就足夠打斷。 Ageing is real, but the timescale is wrong. Six years of secondary school, then university or work, puts the return a decade out. No administration runs a ten-year experiment to find out. Use ageing as the main reason and a single question about timing undoes it.

二 · 題幹給的兩個反對，兩種用法2. The two objections the stem hands you

題幹列了兩組持份者的意見：國際學校營運者怕失去生源，本地家長怕大學學額與就業競爭加劇。 The stem lists two stakeholder views: international school operators fear losing students, local parents fear fiercer competition for university places and jobs.

兩邊的工作量不是對稱的。支持分支要同時完成五件事，而且五件事互相拉扯；反對分支只有一件硬要求：替代方案要站得住。 The workloads are not symmetrical. Support means five jobs that pull against each other. Oppose has one hard requirement: the alternative has to stand up.

三 · 反對比較容易保住一條因果線3. Opposing keeps one causal line

一條線可以這樣走：問題被寫成「學位過剩」，實際是「產品與需求錯配」。所以正解不是放寬收生，而是提升產品（課程、評核、語文要求）並擴大已完成本地化的大學渠道。整篇文章由同一個判斷推出來。 One line runs like this: the problem is framed as surplus places, but it is really a mismatch between product and demand. So the fix is product and channel, not admission rules. Everything in the essay follows from that single judgement.

支持也寫得成，但容易散。Beck 的三項措施就是例子：試點加動態配額、新加坡的服務期與違約金、高才通式的受養人門檻。每一項單獨看都合理，合起來沒有共同的因果，只是三個好主意排在一起。 Support can work, but it drifts. Beck's three features illustrate it: pilot plus dynamic quota, the Singapore bond and service period, a talent-visa dependant gate. Each is sensible on its own. Together they are three good ideas in a row, not one argument.

It's pretty reasonable.」教學錄音，44 分鐘處的問答 "the way I analyze the problem … I don't necessarily think what I just said or the ideas that I came up with is that kind of exceptional. It's pretty reasonable."from the session

這句話值得記住。場上學生問「有時間想這麼多嗎」，答案不是「你要想得更深」，而是這套分析不算特別，只是要快。快是練出來的，不是聰明。 Worth keeping. The student asked whether there is time to think this much. The answer was not that the analysis needs to be deeper, but that it is unremarkable and only needs to be fast. Speed is trained.

四 · 甚麼情況下改寫支持4. When to switch to support

  • 你對政策工具的記憶，比對因果分析強。三項措施你能即刻數出來：配額、門檻、服務期、先導。那就用你的長處。 Your memory for instruments is stronger than your causal reasoning — quota, gate, bond, pilot come to you immediately. Play to that.
  • 你想不到同級的替代方案。只寫得出「加強宣傳」「多做研究」「成立委員會」的話，反對分支會自己塌下來。 You cannot name a comparable alternative. If all you have is "promote more" or "set up a committee", the opposing branch collapses on its own.
  • 數據顯示需求已經在門口。這份卷不成立，非本地生集中在大學；但如果換一份卷，數據顯示已有大量申請，支持就變成順勢。 The data shows demand already queuing. Not true here — non-locals sit at university level — but on a paper where applications are already piling up, support becomes the natural line.

五 · 替代方案的最低要求5. The minimum for an alternative

同一個維度上比較，才叫替代。三個維度：時間、範圍、成本。場上的示範是擴大大學的非本地生名額，理由是三年至四年見效，中學方案要等十年。這是同一條尺上的比較，不是隨手另一個政策。 An alternative is only an alternative if it compares on the same axis: time, scope, cost. The session's example widened the university channel, justified by three or four years to impact against a decade for the school route. Same ruler, not just another idea.

可以考慮的方向：Directions that hold up:

  • 擴大已完成本地化的渠道（大學）。時間短，爭議少，與原方案的範圍相當。Widen the channel that already localises students: universities. Shorter lead time, less conflict, comparable scope.
  • 先導「本地中學國際化」：改課程與評核，再談收生。先動產品，後動資格。Pilot internationalisation of local schools — curriculum and assessment first, admissions second.
  • 針對性處理收生不足：合併、轉型、或把校舍改作其他教育用途。不迴避「有些學校應該結束」這個可能。Handle the empty schools directly: merger, conversion, or repurposing. Do not dodge the possibility that some should close.

六 · 兩份材料的分工6. What each source is good for

教學錄音做的是前提：這個政策想解決的問題，是不是真問題。它沒有走到 Task 2，最後一句是「pass it to AI」。Beck 的復盤做的是其後所有事情：機遇、挑戰、三項措施，寫得完整，但她從未質疑前提。 The session works on the premise — is the problem real — and never reaches Task 2. Beck's recall covers everything after: opportunities, challenges, three features, complete, but without questioning the premise.

七 · 十分鐘怎麼用7. Ten minutes, in practice

看完題，先不要寫。Read the paper, then do not write yet.

  • 0–3 分。抄下題幹說要解決的問題，用它的措辭，不改寫。有兩個動機就抄兩個。 0–3. Copy the problem the stem claims to solve, in its own words. Two motives, two lines.
  • 3–7 分。三問。真嗎（能否指出一個指標）？誰因這個定義而受益？哪個假設未說明（例如收費）？最後一問寫成一句，放在第一段末。 3–7. Three questions. Real — name an indicator. Who benefits from that framing. Which assumption is unstated — fees, for instance. Write the last one as a sentence for the end of paragraph one.
  • 7–10 分。定立場，之後不改。列三個反對者，各寫一句「他怕的具體損失」。這就是 Task 2 措施的來源。 7–10. Commit, then stop re-deciding. List three objectors, one line each on the concrete loss they fear. That is where Task 2's measures come from.

如果十分鐘後你的替代方案仍是空的，翻回支持。這不是失敗，是判斷：反對分支的硬要求在替代方案，不在立場。 If the alternative is still empty at ten minutes, switch to support. That is judgement, not failure: the opposing branch lives or dies on the alternative, not on the stance.
